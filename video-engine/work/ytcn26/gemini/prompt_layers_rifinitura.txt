New signature:
export const GemLayers: React.FC<{ t: number; uid: string; stage: 1 | 2 | 3; cue: number; fx: [number, number] }>
- This component now draws ONLY the illustration (no text at all, no labels, no connector lines, no number badges, no background panel, no dashed separator). Canvas: <svg viewBox="0 0 320 320" width={320} height={320}> with overflow visible. Re-center and scale the garment drawing so the torso fills the canvas nicely (about 250 px tall), with ~30 px free on the left and right for the effects.
- Every id inside <defs> must be prefixed with the `uid` prop (e.g. id={`${uid}-clip0`}) and referenced the same way, so several instances can live on one page.
- Make it a believable RUNNER'S upper body seen from the front: neck, shoulders, and LONG SLEEVES that go down along the body to the wrists (arms slightly away from the torso, hands as simple rounded shapes in skin tone #E7B899; neck in the same skin tone). No head, no face.
- Layers (same colours as now): layer 1 = thin long-sleeved base layer (#B8D7EA) with a round collar; layer 2 = sleeveless insulating vest (#49762E, quilted lines) so the light-blue sleeves and collar stay visible; layer 3 = windproof shell jacket with long sleeves (#F4F1E5, lime #95FF00 zip and edge) whose front is open in a narrow V at the top so a little of the green vest and the blue collar remain visible, and whose hem is slightly shorter than the vest so a green strip shows at the bottom. The layers must read as STACKED.
- `stage` = which layer is being presented: layers with index < stage are already fully drawn from t = 0; the layer with index == stage "zips on" from bottom to top starting at t = cue (0.5 s, ease-out, clipPath reveal) with a small scale pop (1.04 -> 1) when finished; layers above are not drawn.
- Effects, all pure functions of t, looping while active, drawn with rounded strokes/dots:
  stage 1: from t >= fx[0], soft warm glow inside the chest (radial gradient #FFB020, opacity pulsing 0.25-0.45) plus three small heat waves that rise and STAY inside the torso; from t >= fx[1], small sweat droplets (#B8D7EA dots with a white highlight) that travel from the chest OUTWARD through the fabric to the left and right and fade out outside the garment.
  stage 2: from t >= fx[0], the warm glow inside stays and a thick soft warm outline hugs the vest ("heat is held"); from t >= fx[1], thin vapour wisps rise from the shoulders and fade.
  stage 3: from t >= fx[0], five wind streaks (#B8D7EA, dashed) come from the left, hit the jacket and are deflected up/down around it without entering; from t >= fx[1], raindrops fall from the top, hit the shoulders and bounce off as tiny splashes.
- Keep a subtle idle breathing on the whole torso. No hooks, no state, no CSS transitions, no external assets, no text. Strict TypeScript, React 19, `import React from "react"`.
